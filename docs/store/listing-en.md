## Description

Someone will count on you.

Nixo turns quitting into a bond. You adopt a companion, give it a name, and check in your day every evening. Without a cigarette, it grows and changes form. A relapse costs it health. If it goes out, you have three smoke-free days to bring it back.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• A companion that evolves over a year, through six forms, in step with real health milestones.
• Your smoke-free streak, plus freezes for the evenings you forget.
• Your savings live, and a goal to treat yourself with that money.
• A guided two-minute breathing exercise when a craving hits.
• A reminder every evening, at the time you choose.

TOGETHER, YOU HOLD ON
• Your duo sees your streak and can send you strength on hard evenings.
• Your witnesses, people close to you that you accept, can report that they saw you smoke. No cheating.

RESPECTFUL
• No ads, no account to create, no email.
• Your habits stay on your phone.
• Several companions, several species: you choose their names.

Nixo is free. Optional purchases let you bring a companion back right away, buy streak freezes or unlock new species.

Nixo is not a medical device. For support, talk to a health professional or a quitline.
