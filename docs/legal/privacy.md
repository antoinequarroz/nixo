# Nixo privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Nixo is an app that helps you quit smoking by raising a companion. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and how to delete it.

**Controller:** Antoine Quarroz, [postal address], Switzerland. Contact: [contact email].

## In short

- No ads, no data selling, no analytics tools.
- No email or password account: the app creates an anonymous identifier.
- Your habits and journal stay on your phone.
- You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Delete my account.

## What stays on your phone

Cigarettes per day, pack price, journal of checked-in and relapse days, savings goal, companions, reminder settings. This data never leaves your phone.

## What is sent to the server

Only if you use the duo or witnesses:

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Anonymous identifier created by the app | Recognise you without an email or password |
| First name you choose to show | Your duo and witnesses know who you are |
| Name, species, form and health of your active companion, number of smoke-free days | What your duo and witnesses see |
| Invite code, duo and witness links | Run the duo and witness features |
| Reports ("I saw you smoke") and encouragements, with their date | Deliver them to the person concerned |
| Your phone's notification token and language | Notify you when a witness reports you or your duo cheers you on |

Only the people you accepted as your duo or witness can see this information.

**Legal basis:** performing the service you ask for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

## Purchases

Purchases go through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat checks purchases using an anonymous identifier.

## Processors

- **Supabase** (database hosting, region [European Union, to confirm]).
- **Expo** (sends push notifications to Apple and Google).
- **RevenueCat** (purchase checks).
- **Apple** and **Google** (app distribution, payments, notifications).

Some of these services may process data outside Switzerland and the European Union, under the safeguards required by law (standard contractual clauses).

## Retention

Server data is kept while your account exists. Reports and encouragements are deleted with your account, and so are notification tokens.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, object to the processing of, or port your data. **Settings → Delete my account** immediately erases your account and all your data, on the phone and on the server. For any other request, write to [contact email]. You can also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local authority.

## Age

Nixo is meant for adults (18 and over).

## Health

Nixo is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a health professional. The health milestones shown are general guides.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated, and the app will tell you about any important change.
